# Upgrading OpenEMR 8.4.0 to 8.4.1

What changes between `v8_4_0` (2026-09-13) and `v8_4_1` (2026-09-20) that matters before and after an upgrade. The upgrade steps themselves are on the OpenEMR wiki; new features are in the wiki's [Release Features](https://www.open-emr.org/wiki/index.php/Release_Features).

## Before you upgrade

- No change in the PHP version, PHP extensions or Node requirement.
- **Databases CI tested:** 8.4.0: MariaDB 10.11, 11.4, 11.8, 12.3; MySQL 8.4, 9.3 (PHP 8.3, 8.4, 8.5, 8.6). 8.4.1: MariaDB 10.11, 11.4, 11.8, 12.3; MySQL 8.4, 9.3 (PHP 8.3, 8.4, 8.5, 8.6).
